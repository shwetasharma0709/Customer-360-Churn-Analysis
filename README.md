# Customer 360° Analytics & Churn Prediction

An end-to-end **Data Analyst portfolio project** using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze customer behavior, identify churn patterns, segment customers, and prioritize high-risk customers for retention.

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to transform customer-level data into actionable business insights.

The analysis focuses on:

* Customer demographics and behavior
* Customer spending and engagement
* Churn analysis
* Customer segmentation
* Risk scoring
* High-risk customer identification
* Retention opportunities
* Interactive Power BI dashboards

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python** — Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
* **SQL** — MySQL / MySQL Workbench
* **Power BI** — Dashboarding, KPIs, slicers and data visualization
* **GitHub** — Project documentation and portfolio

## 🔍 Key Business Findings

* **500** total customers analyzed
* **94** customers have churned
* Overall churn rate: **18.8%**
* **98** customers are classified as High or Critical risk
* **12** customers are classified as Critical risk
* Critical-risk customers have a **91.7% churn rate**
* Total customer spend is approximately **₹10.70M**
* Average satisfaction score is **3.56 / 5**

### Churn by City

The highest churn rates were observed in:

1. Bhopal — **23.91%**
2. Delhi — **23.44%**
3. Hyderabad — **22.41%**
4. Indore — **19.72%**
5. Bengaluru — **19.35%**

### Churn by Subscription Plan

* Basic — **21.08%**
* Standard — **18.26%**
* Premium — **16.13%**

### Gender Churn

* Male — **20.46%**
* Female — **17.70%**
* Other — **6.67%**

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Prioritize **Critical-risk customers** for immediate retention campaigns.
2. Focus additional attention on customers with high support-ticket volume and low satisfaction.
3. Monitor customers with long periods since their last purchase.
4. Investigate the higher churn observed in Bhopal, Delhi and Hyderabad.
5. Develop targeted retention strategies for the **Basic subscription plan**.
6. Protect high-value customers who are currently classified as High or Critical risk.
